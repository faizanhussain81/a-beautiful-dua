A LETTER FROM MY HEART
✧   TO THE ONE I PRAY FOR   ✧

Dear Shifa,

If there is one thing I wish you could see, it is how much happiness your happiness brings to my heart. Sometimes, I struggle to find the right words to express what I feel, but I hope you always know how special you are to me. You are someone I sincerely pray for, someone whose smile brings peace to my heart, and someone whose happiness means more to me than words can explain.

There is something else I want you to know: you have helped me become a better person in ways you may not even realise. The way you remind me to maintain good relationships with my family, wake me up for Fajr, share your little reminders, your beautiful quotes, and the countless messages you send — all these little things mean more to me than you know. I may not express it every time, but I notice them, I appreciate them, and I carry them in my heart. Thank you for being such a beautiful part of my journey.

Hearing that my entire family was praising you at the function made me genuinely happy and proud. MashaAllah, you deserve every kind word, every bit of appreciation, and every beautiful moment that comes your way. I hope this continues throughout your life, InshaAllah. May Allah fill your days with happiness, respect, and success.

I'm also very happy to hear that you want to do something for your mamma and abbu. I can't promise everything, but InshaAllah, I'll do my very best to support you and your dreams.

And please, always be yourself in front of my family. You don't need to change your personality for anyone. I want you to feel comfortable, respected, and loved exactly as you are.

Always remember that you have me. Even when you feel like there's no one by your side, you're not alone. I might not be much, but I'll always be there for you, through your good days and difficult ones.

I'm praying that these days pass quickly and that we finally meet in person at the right time, InshaAllah. I wish and pray that on the day of our Nikah, we get to have our very first coffee date together, sitting across from each other, finally turning all these little dreams into beautiful memories.❤️

Until then, keep smiling, keep chasing your dreams, and never stop being yourself.

One dua from the bottom of my heart:
O Allah, make her the delight of my eyes in this world and my companion in the Hereafter. Shower her with gardens full of peace, happiness, and endless blessings. Bring us together through a beautiful and blessed Nikah, never let us be separated, and reunite us in Jannah. Ameen. 🤲🏻

With all my love,
Yours, always. ♡
